The Monologue of a Taxpayer as a Father
National Taxation Bureau · 2022 · Grand Prize (金賞)
財政部國稅局 · 2022年 · 首獎（金賞）
Written for the National Taxation Bureau's "Poet Write for You" bilingual poetry competition. Takes the perspective of a taxpaying father reflecting on civic duty and parenthood.
